# Analyst note — NVIDIA platform moat (internal)

**Ticker:** NVDA  
**Year:** 2024  
**Doc type:** analyst_note  
**Author:** Valura research desk  

## Thesis

NVIDIA’s durable advantage is not only GPU silicon. The stack of CUDA, cuDNN, TensorRT, NVLink, and InfiniBand creates a developer and systems moat that competitors must replicate end-to-end.

## Evidence to track

1. Share of training vs inference SKUs in Data Center mix.
2. Networking attach rates (NVLink / InfiniBand) per GPU cluster.
3. Enterprise software (AI Enterprise, NIM) attach as % of Data Center.

## Watch-outs

- Export-control product gaps in China.
- Customer concentration among top hyperscalers.
- HBM and CoWoS packaging bottlenecks delaying Blackwell.

## Cross-ticker question hook

When comparing NVDA and AAPL 10-K risk factors, NVDA emphasizes export controls and supply of advanced packaging; AAPL emphasizes geopolitical concentration of manufacturing and regulatory pressure on Services.
